# Teaching Together at Scale: Lessons from the CodeRefinery Training Network

Delivering hands-on, interactive training at scale is challenging, especially for a geographically distributed and interdisciplinary audience. 
CodeRefinery addresses this with a “bring your own classroom” model that combines shared online teaching with strong local and community-driven support.

CodeRefinery teaches practical, introductory Research Software Engineering skills to researchers across all fields. 
Workshops mix short lectures with hands-on exercises and are streamed live and recorded. 
Participants interact with instructors and helpers via a collaborative document that supports questions, sharing, and feedback. 
All CodeRefinery materials are shared openly so they can be reused and adapted.

To keep workshops community-oriented even with hundreds of participants, local partners host online or in-person classrooms led by a team lead. 
Participants follow the stream together, discuss the content, and work through exercises as a group.

Beyond individual workshops, CodeRefinery also functions as a training network: learners, helpers, team leads, 
and instructors exchange experience and advice on scientific computing and teaching via chat shared with the Nordic-RSE community.
